Compress PDF to 100KB or 200KB on Android

Online application forms for colleges, jobs, scholarships and government services often reject documents that are too big – usually anything over 100KB, 200KB or 500KB. Here is how to shrink a PDF on your phone in seconds, without uploading it to any website.

Steps

  1. Install Scanlo – PDF Scanner, Converter from Google Play (free).
  2. Open Scanlo and choose the Compress PDF tool.
  3. Select the PDF you need to upload (marksheet, certificate, ID proof, resume…).
  4. Pick a compression level. Start with medium; use high if the file is still above the limit.
  5. Save the new file and check its size before uploading to the form.

Tips to get under the limit

  • If you scanned the document, scan it in Black & White or Grayscale – colour scans are much bigger.
  • Remove blank or extra pages before compressing.
  • Photos of documents? Convert them to PDF first, then compress.

Why offline matters

Your certificates and ID documents contain personal data. Scanlo compresses the file on your phone, so nothing is uploaded to a server.
