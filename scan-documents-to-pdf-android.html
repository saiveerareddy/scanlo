Scan documents to PDF with your phone

You don't need a xerox shop or a scanner machine to make a PDF of a document. Your phone camera is enough.

Steps

  1. Install Scanlo from Google Play.
  2. Open the Scan tool and point the camera at the page in good light.
  3. Scanlo detects the page edges and straightens it automatically. Adjust the corners if needed.
  4. Choose a filter – Sharper or Black & White make text easy to read.
  5. Add more pages if you have them, then save everything as one PDF.

What you can scan

  • Assignments and handwritten notes
  • Aadhaar, PAN and other ID cards (front and back on one page)
  • Receipts and bills
  • Certificates, marksheets and forms
  • Book pages and whiteboards

Tips for a clean scan

  • Place the paper on a dark, flat surface.
  • Avoid shadows – hold the phone directly above the page.
  • Use OCR in Scanlo if you need to copy text from the scan.
