Merge multiple PDFs into one on Android

Many forms let you upload only one file, but your documents are in several PDFs. Merge them into a single PDF on your phone.

Steps

  1. Install Scanlo from Google Play.
  2. Open the Merge PDF tool.
  3. Select all the PDFs you want to combine.
  4. Drag to put them in the right order.
  5. Merge and save – you now have one PDF.

Related tools

  • Split PDF – take out only the pages you need.
  • Compress PDF – make the merged file small enough to upload. See how to compress PDF to 200KB.
  • Rotate, reorder and delete pages before sharing.
