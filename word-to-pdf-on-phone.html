Convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF on your phone

Teachers, offices and online portals usually ask for PDF files. If your assignment or resume is in Word, or your report is in Excel or PowerPoint, you can convert it on your phone in a few taps.

Steps

  1. Install Scanlo from Google Play.
  2. Open the converter and choose Word to PDF, Excel to PDF or PowerPoint to PDF.
  3. Select your .doc/.docx, .xls/.xlsx/.csv or .ppt/.pptx file.
  4. Convert and save the PDF – then share it on WhatsApp, email or upload it.

Also works the other way

Need to edit a PDF? Scanlo can also convert PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint or JPG.

No internet needed

Conversion happens on your phone, so it works offline and your files stay private.
