Privacy Policy

Scanlo: PDF Scanner & Converter · Effective date: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Scanlo: PDF Scanner & Converter Android app ("Scanlo", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Scanlo is developed by Veera Reddy S. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your documents stay on your device

All document features — scanning, PDF conversion (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images), editing, merging, splitting, compression, password protection, redaction, OCR, translation, summarising and comparison — are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, store, or share your documents, scanned images, or the text they contain. We have no servers that receive your files.

2. Permissions we use

  • Camera – to scan documents. Images are captured and processed only on your device.
  • Files and storage / media access – to open the documents you choose and save the files you create. We only access files you select or create in the app.
  • Internet – used to display ads and to send crash reports. It is not used to transfer your documents.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Scanlo is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interactions and app usage information
  • Diagnostic information

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Crash reports and diagnostics

To keep the app stable, we may collect crash logs and diagnostic data (such as device type, OS version and the technical details of the error). This data does not include your documents and is used only to fix bugs and improve performance.

5. How we use and share information

Information described above is used only to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and fix crashes. We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers named in this policy (Google AdMob and our crash reporting provider) or when required by law.

6. Data security and retention

Data collected by third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections and retained according to those providers' policies. Files you create remain on your device until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes app data stored by Scanlo.

7. Children's privacy

Scanlo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can control permissions from your device settings, limit ad personalisation, and reset your Advertising ID. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data; because we do not store your documents or personal data on our own servers, requests about ad data may be directed to Google.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the developer email address shown on Scanlo's Google Play store listing.
